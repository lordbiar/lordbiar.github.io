Who we are

Our purpose

Technology is not a privilege but a necessity for education, employment, and sustainable development.

Vision

To build digitally empowered communities where every child and young person has equal access to technology, quality digital education, and opportunities to achieve their full potential.

Mission

To transform lives by providing accessible digital education, technology resources, and innovative learning opportunities that equip refugees and underserved communities with skills for education, employment, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.

What we do

Core programs

Practical, hands-on training and resources designed for real-world impact.

Digital skills

Basic computer skills training

  • Computer fundamentals
  • Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint
  • Internet and email
  • Digital communication
  • Online safety and cybersecurity
For: Youth, women, adults, job seekers, refugees, host community members
School access

Computer access for school children

  • Computers for CBC Grade 6 to Grade 9
  • Digital literacy improvement
  • Online research skills
  • Technology confidence building
  • Preparation for a digital future
For: School children in underserved communities
AI literacy

Basic artificial intelligence

  • Introduction to AI concepts
  • Everyday AI applications
  • Responsible AI use
  • AI tools for education
  • AI for productivity and creativity
For: Learners of all ages
Web development

Website development

  • HTML and CSS fundamentals
  • Basic JavaScript
  • Responsive web design
  • Website publishing
  • Personal portfolio development
For: Young people seeking employable tech skills
